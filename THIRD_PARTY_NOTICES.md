# Third-Party Notices & Attribution

MenoMate Core incorporates algorithmic designs and menstrual tracking concepts inspired by the following open-source projects, implemented independently in Python:

---

## 1. Mensinator
- **Repository**: https://github.com/EmmaTellblom/Mensinator
- **License**: MIT License
- **Inspiration**:
  - Deliberately simple, non-bloated period and symptom tracking architecture.
  - Relational child symptom design and semantic symptom identifiers.
  - Transparent statistical tracking without artificial data fabrication.

---

## 2. Mētra
- **Repository**: https://github.com/paolo-santucci/metra
- **License**: GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPL-3.0)
- **Notice**:
  - **No source code from Mētra was copied or directly incorporated into MenoMate.**
  - Mētra's published documentation and algorithmic methodology inspired the independent implementation of a transparent weighted moving average (WMA) prediction engine over recent completed cycles, cycle variability tracking, and privacy-first data minimization principles.

---

## 3. YIMA
- **Repository**: https://github.com/Novawerk/YIMA
- **Inspiration**:
  - Period start and end logging semantics.
  - Onboarding fallbacks for unknown cycle lengths ("I'm not sure").
  - Clear separation between bleeding occurrences and multi-week cycle calculations.
